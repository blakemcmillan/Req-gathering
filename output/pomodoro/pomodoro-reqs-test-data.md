# Pomodoro Timer — Requirements Gathering Test Data

## Overwhelmed Office Worker

### Task 1: Start a focused work session without distractions

**Gains:**
- Dedicated uninterrupted time to tackle my most important task
- Built-in permission to ignore emails and notifications for 25 minutes

**Pains:**
- Too many apps and notifications constantly interrupt my flow
- Hard to remember when I last took a break, so I get mentally fatigued mid-afternoon

### Task 2: Track which tasks consume most of my focus time

**Gains:**
- Visibility into where my day actually goes instead of just feeling scattered
- Data to push back on unrealistic meeting schedules that steal focus time

**Pains:**
- Switching between tasks kills momentum and makes tracking tedious
- No clear picture of whether I'm spending time on priorities or just reacting

---

## Focused Software Developer

### Task 1: Maintain deep flow state across multiple complex tasks

**Gains:**
- Structured breaks that actually restore focus rather than just checking Slack
- Clear measurement of sustainable focus sessions vs. days where I'm running on fumes

**Pains:**
- Standard 25-minute sessions are too short for deep problem-solving work
- Not all breaks are equal—some days I need physical movement, other days I need a coffee break

### Task 2: Analyze weekly focus patterns to optimize my schedule

**Gains:**
- Data-driven insights to identify which times of day I'm most productive
- Ability to protect my peak focus hours from meetings

**Pains:**
- Have to manually track which sessions were high-quality focus vs. shallow work
- No breakdown of task complexity vs. time spent—can't tell if I'm slow or just working on hard problems

### Task 3: Integrate Pomodoro tracking with existing task management

**Gains:**
- Single system of record so focus time is tied directly to outcomes delivered
- Fewer context switches pulling attention away from deep work

**Pains:**
- Logging focus sessions separately from task completion creates duplicate data entry
- Hard to correlate Pomodoro counts with actual task progress

## Student Balancing Multiple Courses

### Task 1: Study effectively without burning out

**Gains:**
- Structured approach that feels sustainable rather than cramming all night
- Clear guidance on when to take breaks so I stay alert during study sessions

**Pains:**
- Unsure if I should study one subject for multiple sessions or switch between subjects
- Pressure to maximize study time, but not sure if longer sessions actually work better for learning

### Task 2: Compare study time across subjects and exams

**Gains:**
- Visibility into which subjects are eating up time so I can adjust preparation strategy
- Evidence of study effort to identify where I'm actually struggling vs. where I need different techniques

**Pains:**
- No good way to track study time across different devices and study sessions
- Difficulty matching study hours to actual exam performance to see what studying tactics work
